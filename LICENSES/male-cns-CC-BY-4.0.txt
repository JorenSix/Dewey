Derived material in this repository:
app/Sources/FlyApp/Resources/circuit.json is ADAPTED from the flat-connectome files
(body annotations, neurotransmitter predictions, connectome weights; minconf 0.5) by
pipeline/build_circuit.py. Changes: a selection of behaviour-relevant cell types and
their strongest one-hop partners, neurons collapsed to (cell type, side) nodes, and
weights converted to signed fractions of each node's total input. Exact source files
and SHA-256 hashes: pipeline/data_manifest.json. The raw files are not redistributed.
